Tank Monitor — 4.3″ panel, three design directions

Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-4.3 · 800×480 · rendered 1:1. Physical face ≈ 94 × 56 mm. All three share one system: deep cool-black ground, a single elevation layer (inset top highlight + drop shadow — LVGL does both natively), wide-tracked uppercase micro-labels, and tight negative letter-spacing on the numerals. Heat is orange, cool is blue, and relays read ON/OFF, never a percentage.

1 · Orbit — radial gauge + bento

The arc isn't decoration: it shows where in the safe band you are, with the setpoint as a tick. Diverging blue→neutral→orange, so drift reads as direction before you parse the digits.

3 · Monolith — restraint

One number, sized so it reads from the far side of the room, and nothing competing with it. Everything else is one quiet row; the rest lives on swipe pages. Modern by subtraction — and the most legible at a glance, at 6am, from a doorway.
